📊 Percentage Mastery Notes

Learn the basics of percentages before solving problems ✨

⭐ What is Percentage?

Percentage means "per hundred". The word percentage comes from the Latin word percentum, meaning out of 100.

For example:

50% = 50 out of 100 = 50/100 = 1/2


⭐ Basic Percentage Formula

Whenever you need to find what percentage one value is of another:

\[ \displaystyle \boxed{\text{Percentage}=\frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Whole}}\times 100} \]

Example:

A student scores 360 marks out of 450.

Percentage =

\[ \displaystyle \frac{360}{450}\times 100 = 80\% \]

⭐ Finding Percentage Increase

When a value increases:

\[ \displaystyle \boxed{\text{Percentage Increase}=\frac{\text{New}-\text{Original}}{\text{Original}}\times 100} \]

Example:

A phone price increases from ₹10000 to ₹12000.

Increase = 12000 - 10000 = 2000

Percentage increase:

\[ \displaystyle \frac{2000}{10000}\times 100 = 20\% \]

⭐ Finding Percentage Decrease

\[ \displaystyle \boxed{\text{Percentage Decrease}=\frac{\text{Original}-\text{New}}{\text{Original}}\times 100} \]

Example:

A shirt price decreases from ₹800 to ₹600.

Decrease = 200

\[ \displaystyle \frac{200}{800}\times 100 = 25\% \]

⭐ Important Percentage Conversions

  • 50% = 1/2
  • 25% = 1/4
  • 75% = 3/4
  • 20% = 1/5
  • 10% = 1/10

🔥 CLAT Percentage Tricks

  • Always identify the original value first.
  • Increase and decrease percentages are calculated on the original value.
  • A 20% increase followed by 20% decrease does NOT return to the original value.
  • For multiple percentage changes, use:
\[ \displaystyle \boxed{\text{Final Value}=\text{Original}\times \left(1+\frac{x}{100}\right)\times \left(1-\frac{y}{100}\right)} \]

💯 Master Percentages Step by Step 💯

📊 Percentage Challenge

Question 1

A student scored 360 marks out of 500 in the half-yearly examination. In the final examination, the total marks remained the same, but the student improved the score by 50 marks.


By how many percentage points did the student's percentage increase?






💡 Solution

⭐ Formula

Percentage = Obtained Marks ÷ Total Marks × 100

Step 1

Original Percentage

= 360 ÷ 500 × 100

= 72%

Step 2

New Marks

360 + 50 = 410

New Percentage

410 ÷ 500 × 100

= 82%

Step 3

Increase in percentage points

82 − 72 = 10 percentage points

✅ Correct Answer: b) 10 percentage points

💡 CLAT Tip

Don't confuse percentage with percentage points. Here, 72% → 82% The increase is 10 percentage points, not 10% of 72.

Question 2

A shopkeeper increases the price of a bicycle by 25%. A month later, due to low demand, he offers a 20% discount on the increased price.

Compared to the original price, what is the overall change in the price?


Select the correct answer.






💡 Solution

⭐ Important Formula

Final Value = Original Value × (1 + Increase%) × (1 − Decrease%)

Step 1

Assume the original price is ₹100.

Step 2

After a 25% increase:

₹100 → ₹125

Step 3

20% discount on ₹125

20% of ₹125 = ₹25

New price = 125 − 25 = ₹100

Final price = Original price

Therefore, there is no overall change.

✅ Correct Answer: b) Same as the original price

💡 CLAT Tip

Always apply the second percentage to the new value, not the original one. Using ₹100 as an assumed value is often the quickest way to solve percentage questions.

Question 3

A batsman had already scored 240 runs in a tournament. In the next match, he scored 60 more runs.

By what percentage did his total runs increase?







💡 Solution

⭐ Formula

Percentage Increase = Increase ÷ Original Value × 100

Step 1

Increase in runs = 60

Step 2

Percentage Increase = (60 ÷ 240) × 100

= 0.25 × 100

= 25%

✅ Correct Answer: b) 25%

💡 Remember Always divide by the original value, not the new value.

Question 4

A water tank was 40% full. After adding 300 litres of water, the tank became 70% full.

What is the total capacity of the tank?







💡 Solution

⭐ Formula

Capacity = Increase in Water ÷ Increase in Percentage

Tank increased from 40% to 70%

Increase = 30%

30% of tank = 300 litres

100% of tank = 300 × 100 ÷ 30

= 1000 litres

✅ Correct Answer: c) 1000 litres

💡 CLAT Tip

Whenever a percentage changes by a known quantity, that quantity represents only the difference in percentage, not the whole.

Question 5

In a class, 60% of the students are girls. If there are 18 boys in the class, how many girls are there?







💡 Solution

⭐ Formula

Whole = Part × 100 ÷ Percentage

Step 1

Girls = 60%

Therefore, Boys = 40%

Step 2

40% = 18 students

100% = 18 × 100 ÷ 40

= 45 students

Step 3

Girls = 60% of 45

= 45 × 60 ÷ 100

= 27 girls

✅ Correct Answer: b) 27

💡 CLAT Tip

If one percentage is given, always find the remaining percentage first. Here, Girls = 60% so Boys = 40%.

Question 6

A private company revised the salaries of its employees. During the first quarter, every employee received a 15% salary increment. However, due to financial constraints in the second quarter, the revised salary was reduced by 10%.

After both revisions, an employee's monthly salary became ₹62,100.

What was the employee's original monthly salary before any revision?







💡 Solution

⭐ CLAT Trick

Whenever two percentage changes happen one after another, work backwards from the final value.

Step 1

Let the original salary be x.

Step 2

After a 15% increase:

Salary = 1.15x

Step 3

A 10% reduction means only 90% remains.

Final Salary = 1.15x × 0.90

= 1.035x

Step 4

1.035x = 62100

x = 62100 ÷ 1.035

x = ₹60,000

✅ Correct Answer: b) ₹60,000

💡 Exam Tip

Instead of finding 15% and then 10% separately, convert them into multipliers: +15% → ×1.15
−10% → ×0.90 This is much faster and avoids calculation mistakes.

Question 7

A health department surveyed 2,500 people in a town.

  • 72% received the first vaccine dose.
  • Among those people, 80% completed the second dose.
  • Among those who completed both doses, 25% also received a booster dose.

How many people received all three doses?







💡 Solution

⭐ CLAT Tip

Read every sentence carefully. Each percentage is applied to the previous group, not the total population.

Step 1

First dose = 72% of 2500

= 2500 × 72 ÷ 100

= 1800 people

Step 2

Second dose = 80% of 1800

= 1800 × 80 ÷ 100

= 1440 people

Step 3

Booster = 25% of 1440

= 1440 × 25 ÷ 100

= 360 people

✅ Correct Answer: c) 360

💡 Remember

Never apply every percentage directly to the original total. Always identify which group each percentage refers to.

Question 8

According to the latest census report, the population of Greenfield City increased by 20% between 2022 and 2023.

During the following year, migration caused the population to decrease by 10%.

If the population at the end of 2024 was 32,400, what was the population in 2022?







💡 Solution

⭐ Shortcut

Convert every percentage into a multiplier.

+20% → ×1.20
−10% → ×0.90

Step 1

Overall multiplier = 1.20 × 0.90

= 1.08

Step 2

Original Population × 1.08 = 32,400

Step 3

Original Population = 32400 ÷ 1.08

= 30,000

✅ Correct Answer: c) 30,000

💡 CLAT Tip

For successive percentage changes, never add or subtract the percentages directly. Always multiply the percentage factors.

Question 9

A student appeared for an examination. He secured 45% marks and failed by 15 marks. The minimum passing requirement for the examination is 50%.

Find the maximum marks of the examination.







💡 Solution

⭐ CLAT Shortcut

Whenever the difference between two percentages is given in marks, first find what percentage that difference represents.

Step 1

Passing Percentage = 50%

Student's Percentage = 45%

Difference = 5%

Step 2

5% = 15 marks

Step 3

100% = 15 × 100 ÷ 5

= 300 marks

✅ Correct Answer: b) 300

💡 Remember

The student did not score 15 marks. The student scored 15 marks less than the passing marks. Those 15 marks represent the missing 5%.

Question 10

A pharmaceutical company manufactured 8,000 bottles of cough syrup. After quality testing:

  • 12% bottles were rejected due to packaging defects.
  • Of the remaining bottles, 25% were exported.
  • The remaining bottles were supplied to hospitals across the country.

How many bottles were supplied to hospitals?







💡 Solution

⭐ CLAT Tip

Every percentage is applied to the remaining bottles, not the original total.

Step 1

Rejected bottles = 12% of 8000

= 8000 × 12 ÷ 100 = 960

Step 2

Remaining bottles

= 8000 − 960 = 7040

Step 3

Exported bottles

= 25% of 7040

= 7040 × 25 ÷ 100 = 1760

Step 4

Supplied to hospitals

= 7040 − 1760

= 5280 bottles

✅ Correct Answer: c) 5280

💡 Remember

Read the passage carefully. The second percentage is applied only after removing the rejected bottles. This is a very common CLAT interpretation trap.
