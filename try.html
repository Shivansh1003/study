About The Club

Cultivating Tech Excellence at MITS Gwalior

GSX Student Chapter is a technology and innovation hub at MITS Gwalior designed to inspire, educate, and elevate student developers into industry-ready contributors.

We provide an open, vibrant platform where curious minds collaborate on real-world software, participate in hackathons, and master in-demand technical disciplines. As an extension of the GirlScript Foundation, we are on a mission to democratize technology education and champion diversity across engineering domains.
